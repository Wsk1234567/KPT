Kawasan Pahang Tengah is the Central Pahang area of St. John Ambulans Malaysia - a non-profit uniformed movement serving communities through first aid, ambulance support, youth development and public education.

Who we are

Service for mankind, on the ground in Pahang.

St. John Ambulance of Malaysia is a leading NGO providing ambulance service, first aid training, event health services and community care. Through Cadets and Juniors in school Divisions, we also run youth development programmes nationwide.

In Kawasan Pahang Tengah, volunteers serve at events, support ambulance duties and train the public across Central Pahang.

We are part of a movement rooted in the Order of St. John - known globally for first aid, care and disciplined service - and recognised in Malaysian law under Act No. 74.

History

From a chivalric order to the roadside.

1877 - 1888

The Order of St. John

St. John Ambulance Association (1877) and Brigade (1888) take shape in Britain. In 1888 Queen Victoria recognises the Order as a British Order of Chivalry by Royal Charter - the crest and name become protected symbols of service.

1908

Roots in Malaya

St. John Ambulance work begins in the region, growing through first aid training, nursing and public duty in towns and schools.

1938 - 1945

Service through crisis

Members serve through wartime and recovery years - training, casualty support and community resilience under pressure.

1957 - 1971

Localisation

After independence, the movement deepens local leadership, school Divisions and public programmes across the new nation.

1972

St. John Ambulance of Malaysia

Parliament Act No. 74 (Incorporation Act) unites the movement nationally and protects the St. John name and emblem from unauthorised use.

2000s - today

Modern service

ISO 9001-certified operations, expanded ambulance fleet, dialysis and community care, and one of the country's largest volunteer first-aid networks.

Legal notice

Protected name and emblem.

The names "St. John Ambulance of Malaysia", "St. John Ambulans Malaysia" and the emblem are protected under the Laws of Malaysia and other laws where St. John Ambulance or the Order of St. John exists. Unauthorised use is prohibited.
