Services

Four services.
One standard of care.

Clear offerings for the public, organisers, schools and corporate partners in Central Pahang.

01

Ambulance Service

Who it is for: Hospitals, care homes, families, event organisers

Emergency and non-emergency ambulance transport with trained crews. Includes patient transfer support, festive-season standby on major routes, and coordination with event medical teams.

Request transfer ->
02

Event Health Services

Who it is for: Races, festivals, schools, corporate days

First aid posts, medical standby and trained volunteers for races, concerts, school meets, festivals and corporate days. Crew size, equipment and evacuation routes planned to your risk profile.

Book coverage ->
03

First Aid Training

Who it is for: Public, workplaces, schools

Public awareness sessions and certified courses for workplaces, schools and communities. Practical skills under pressure: CPR, AED, bleeding, choking and fracture response.

View courses ->
04

Community Services

Who it is for: Residents, partners, agencies

Health awareness, blood donation support, nursing and dialysis-linked initiatives, disaster readiness, and partnerships with local agencies for continuous care.

How it works

From enquiry to duty.

01

Tell us the brief

Date, venue, crowd size, risk notes - ambulance, first aid post or trainers.

02

Get a plan

We propose crew, equipment and fees (or community support options where applicable).

03

Confirm & prepare

Roster locked, equipment checked, on-site briefing with your organiser.

04

On duty

Professional coverage on the day - and a short report after major events if required.

For organisers

Need coverage for an event?

Tell us the date, venue, crowd size and risk notes. We’ll propose a crew and equipment plan.
